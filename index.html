Me in 10 seconds

Systems person, product marketer, runner, dad, and a firm believer that a well-built document can change what a group of people is able to do.

(school years) Trumpet player in the high school band. Sat in a gym this fall watching my daughter's friend play trumpet and was right back there.

(work) Senior product marketing manager at Airgain, working from home. My current obsession: a repeatable, data-driven win/loss program that turns lost deals into faster wins.

(community) Co-chair of the New Westminster DPAC. I build the infographics, meeting materials, and systems behind the scenes so other parents and PACs have what they need to be effective. Systems built right and shared are a force enabler.

(running) Marathon PB of 3:40. Chasing sub-3:30 and a Boston qualification at the Victoria Marathon in October 2027 — by way of HYROX this December and the BMO Half next May.

(home) Husband to Nicole, dad to Alex (19) and Ellie (17), and staff to Finley, a goldendoodle who runs the walking schedule.

Me in 10 minutes?

Short version: I like taking messy, recurring problems and turning them into systems other people can run without me. It is the same instinct whether the problem is a sales team's lost deals, a school district's parent communication, or my own training plan. The longer version is in the writing below — that's where I think out loud.

What am I doing now?

  • Training: final block before HYROX men's doubles on December 20, 2026 — strength twice a week, intervals Wednesdays, back primer every morning.
  • Work: standing up the win/loss program at Airgain with our sales directors — interview cadence, a shared evidence base, and findings the team actually uses.
  • Community: DPAC season — board updates, meeting packages, and making nwdpac.ca more of a hub for PACs and parents.
  • Writing: a nightly Homework for Life entry — one story-worthy moment, one gratitude, one stress, one thing learned — and shaping the best of them into posts.
  • Quietly: trying to sleep more than six hours a night. It is going about as well as you'd expect.
